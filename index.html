    Specimen 04 · Methods

    Field notes

    Nothing on this page is keyframed. Every pattern is a simulation running in your browser, using published models.

    Travelling-wave growth

    θ ← θ + σξ,   ξ ~ N(0,1)
x ← x + ℓ(cos θ, sin θ)
branch with p = 0.022 / step
fuse on contact → tip stops

    Tips random-walk, branch and fuse. No global rule exists, yet the colony advances as a wave with constant density behind the front.

    Oyarte Gálvez et al., Nature 639 (2025) · Hannezo et al., Cell 171 (2017)

    Adaptive tubes

    Σⱼ (Dᵢⱼ/Lᵢⱼ)(pᵢ − pⱼ) = Iᵢ
Qᵢⱼ = (Dᵢⱼ/Lᵢⱼ)(pᵢ − pⱼ)
dDᵢⱼ/dt = f(|Qᵢⱼ|) − Dᵢⱼ
f(Q) = Qᵞ / (1 + Qᵞ)

    Kirchhoff flow through a mesh, solved each frame with conjugate gradients. Used tubes thicken and idle ones decay.

    Tero et al., Science 327, 439 (2010)

    Two-way streaming

    v ∝ Q   (sign = direction)
source, sink resampled
every iteration

    Particles ride the flux. Because sources change, flow reverses along the same tubes, like the bidirectional cytoplasmic traffic filmed in fungal hyphae.

    Oyarte Gálvez et al., Nature 639 (2025)

    Tube radius

    D ∝ r⁴   (Hagen–Poiseuille)
r³ = Σ rᵢ³   (Murray's law)

    Conductivity grows with the fourth power of radius, which is why trunks are few and thick. Line width here is drawn as √D, exaggerated for legibility.

    Murray, PNAS 12 (1926)

    Chemotropism

    θ ← θ + κ·sin(φ − θ)·(1 − d/R)

    Hyphae respond to chemical signals from roots. Here your cursor plays the root: nearby tips steer toward it.
